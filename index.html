BreakOut

PoC · 12 + 12 → 64

PoC — sample winners are deterministic so the structure is comparable across reloads. Two 12-team brackets feed losers into the 64-team consolation at staggered rounds; the 64-team alternates "integrate losers" / "winners advance" through to the final. Loser destinations and entry sources are annotated on every match.
